Notes

This feature removes subjective words such as pronouns (I, me, you, they, etc.) from the transcript to make the text more objective. By eliminating personal references, it helps in creating a more neutral and unbiased representation of the video content, which is useful for general analysis and summarization.

Summary

This feature uses Latent Semantic Analysis (LSA) to generate a concise version of the transcript. By analyzing the relationships between words and sentences, it extracts the most relevant content and reduces the transcript to two key sentences, making it easier to grasp the main idea of the video without going through the entire transcript.

Key Points

This feature utilizes spaCy to analyze the transcript and extract informative sentences. It ensures that only meaningful statements containing more than five words are retained, filtering out less important content. This helps in identifying the essential parts of the transcript while maintaining context and coherence.
